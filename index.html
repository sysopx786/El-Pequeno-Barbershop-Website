About El Pequeño

The little shop at 603.

El Pequeño Barbershop stands out as a premier destination for grooming in Reading, PA, known for its exceptional service and welcoming atmosphere. Renowned for its skilled barbers, this shop excels at high-quality haircuts tailored to each client — so everyone leaves satisfied and confident.

The shop is clean, organized, and comfortable for all ages. Families come for kid-friendly cuts. The staff is bilingual, and they take the time to understand what you want — especially high fades, done with precision.

That hospitality is why people keep coming back. It’s more than a barbershop: a place to relax and connect, with professionalism and a personal touch. Ask for Julian. Ask for Gabriel. Or take the next open chair.
